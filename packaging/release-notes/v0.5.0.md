## Music without a key

Pitch-class sets, micromodes and twelve-tone rows get the tools that major and minor keys already had.

- **Forms and degrees.** In a key that is a set or a micromode, the harmony keys show its forms, each with its degree (**Tn**, **TnI**), the way a major key shows I, ii and V. They come home first and then by how many notes they keep, and progressions of forms can be played and written on the chord sheet.
- **Any set as the key.** The key menu lists every pitch-class set by size, from 2 to 10 notes, upright or inverted, and outlines the one the song uses most.
- **Changing the set keeps the plan.** Switching the key to another set or micromode keeps every form's degree, the way C major to C minor keeps every chord's.
- **The band plays it.** A micromode style plays the forms written on the chord sheet, and a twelve-tone style plays your row in the forms written there (`P0 | RI5 | I7`). The Row window takes a plan of forms, and the piano roll can turn notes backwards and upside down.
- **Hearing it.** Voice leading checks the colour of a set or micromode. *Find the key* also names a passage's colour. The chord sheet can read forms that mix chords and lines, and ranks the sets a song uses most. *Ear training* has three new tabs: micromodes, forms of a set and forms of a row.

## The band and the Library

- **Forty new styles**, 217 in all, with 141 drum rhythms: jazz and blues, marches, classical dances, Latin and South American rhythms, and more.
- The Library groups styles by genre, has a **Swing** filter and an **instrument menu**, and finds styles by instrument, in English and Spanish.
- **The drums follow the song's sections.** Where a song names no sections, as on a demo or a MIDI file, they are heard in its notes.
- A style applied to an imported **MIDI file** gives the file's own players its sounds, not only the band's.

## Recording and editing

- **Takes.** Sing or whistle a part while the song plays; the app writes what it heard as notes on the Lead or any other track, as sung, in the key, or following the chords, and keeps the recording on its own track.
- **Trimming clips.** Drag a clip's edges on the playlist to hide its start or end, pattern clips and recordings alike. The notes stay in the pattern.
- **Windows** open as they were when the app was closed.
