## Harmony, sheets and the band

- **Current time signature under the Time menu.** When a song starts with a pickup bar or changes meter along the way, the Time menu now shows the time signature where the music is (`Now in …`), matching how the Key menu shows the current key.
- **More accurate harmony and voice leading.** Slash chords with a non-chord bass note keep their bass note in chord names and descriptions, minor-key raised sevenths spell the leading tone in harmonic minor, voice leading checks leading-tone and seventh resolutions and voice crossings accurately, scale spelling avoids triple accidentals, and key finding handles silent and modal passages more reliably.
- **Chord sheet and lead sheets.** Polychords (such as `Eb7|Fb`) survive text export and import without breaking unspaced bar lines (`|C|G|Am|F|`). Transposed lead sheets pick cleaner enharmonic keys for B-flat, E-flat and F instruments, handle key changes and dotted/tied rhythms cleanly, and cope better with irregular meters.
- **Band comping and voicings.** Afterbeat comping in 3/4 and 6/8, guitar capo voicings and horn sections handle meter and phrasing edge cases cleanly.

## Sound, takes, video and files

- **Sturdier playback and audio engine.** Real-time audio rendering, channel effects (equalizer, compressor and delay) and SoundFont playback avoid glitches and allocations on the audio thread.
- **Takes and audio import.** Singing or whistling takes track pitch and timing more accurately across octave jumps, and audio decoding guards against damaged WAV or MP3 files.
- **Video export.** MP4/H.264 export validates frame dimensions, timing and audio synchronization cleanly, and the export dialog gives clearer defaults and feedback.
- **Safer saving and editing.** Atomic project saves, corrupted-file checks, MIDI import, mixer controls, the style editor and undo/redo history fix edge cases and keep project state consistent.

## Interface, demos and documentation

- **Clearer controls and Spanish wording.** Disabled buttons explain why they are inactive on hover, keyboard navigation and the time-signature picker behave more smoothly, and missing Spanish translations are filled in.
- **Demos and user manual.** *Cielito lindo* moves to Latin American folklore next to *Jesusita en Chihuahua*, and manual chapter titles match the app's groups in English and Spanish.
- **Updated books and PDFs.** Both user manuals and both companion books are synced with the latest app behavior, and PDF generation works again on newer Chrome.
