# Ideas for what comes next

None of these are planned yet. They are ideas, roughly from smaller to
bigger, and each would make a good project for a reader of this book:

- **More of the band**: more styles, and more instruments it can bring
  to any of them, as it brings the horns now ([A horn
  section](../band/band.md#a-horn-section)).
- **A soloist that is no longer an experiment** ([The
  soloist](../band/soloist.md)).
- **Recording in song mode**: today Rec switches to pattern mode and
  records into the pattern that loops ([exercise 22 of Part
  II](../sound/exercises.md#22-record-in-song-mode-all-three-crates-harder)).
- **Audio clips** on the tracks, next to the patterns, placed and moved
  like any clip. A take's recording now plays as one sampler note on
  its first step, in a pattern of its own, which the engine starts
  partway when playback starts later ([Starting
  partway](../sound/instruments.md#starting-partway)).
- **Beats, keys and chords heard in a recording**, so a band could
  follow a rhythmic or harmonic recording added to the song, as takes
  follow a sung tune ([Takes](../theory/listen.md)).
- **Automation**: knob movements recorded or drawn over time, on the
  tracks.
