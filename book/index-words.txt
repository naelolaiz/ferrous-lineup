# the id of its heading. A word of a glossary listed here keeps its
# glossary page and gets these too.
#
#     Word: see Another word
#
# sends the reader to another entry. And a word that a chapter writes in
# bold with another meaning (the Rust "function" for the music one) is
# left out of that chapter with "not":
#
#     Function: not program/sync.md
